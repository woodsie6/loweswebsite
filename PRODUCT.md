# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Council, education, and public-estate decision-makers across North Wales and North West England who need a dependable contractor for construction, planned maintenance, or contracted reactive response.

## Product Purpose

Explain Lowes Building Services Limited's capabilities and public-sector experience, establish procurement confidence, and make it easy for qualified prospective clients to contact the team for a project or maintenance enquiry.

## Brand Personality

Established, direct, accountable. The site should feel calm under pressure, operationally credible, and personal enough that visitors expect to reach a decision-maker rather than a call centre.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic domestic-builder templates, exaggerated emergency-callout language, anonymous corporate procurement portals, and decorative effects that distract from evidence, services, or contact routes.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with specific operational proof rather than broad claims.
2. Keep direct contact routes visible and functional without relying on enhancement JavaScript.
3. Use motion to explain progress and craft, never to gate content.
4. Preserve a clear distinction between contracted reactive cover and ad-hoc emergency work.
5. Make every trust claim easy for public-sector buyers to verify.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in practical implementation. Preserve full keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic links and navigation, reduced-motion behavior, responsive text, and functional core content when JavaScript is unavailable.
